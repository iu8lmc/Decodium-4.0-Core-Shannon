## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 — v1.0.396 · Core Gallager**
🧬 New name + deep weak-signal dig

🛠 **What changed**
• **New name: "Core Gallager"** — Decodium's core is now named after **Robert G. Gallager**, the inventor of LDPC codes (1960), the math at the heart of FT8/FT4 decoding. Only the visible name changes (splash, Info screen): no impact on your setup.
• **New "GAL" button** (next to Monitor) — turns on a **deep weak-signal dig**: a second decoding pass parallelized across your CPU cores, recovering stations near the noise floor that normal decoding leaves behind. In on-air tests it brings Decodium **on par with or beyond the best decoders** on weak signals, while staying within the cycle timing and without disturbing receive audio.
• ⚠️ **OFF by default.** It needs a multi-core CPU: on older PCs it can strain the audio → in that case leave it off (the button tooltip reminds you). Switch it on when you want to squeeze the weak ones.
