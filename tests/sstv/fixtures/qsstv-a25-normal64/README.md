# QSSTV A/2.5 normal 64-QAM short A/B fixtures

The zlib-compressed files contain 12 kHz mono signed 16-bit little-endian
PCM resampled with FFmpeg from two 48 kHz stereo WAVs generated by locally
instrumented QSSTV-Experimental revision `a84584248d3307bfa081362031ff20f365337633`.
That transmitter includes a signed-PCM correction and hybrid instrumentation;
hybrid was disabled. The unmodified pinned QSSTV 10.0 receiver identified the
original WAVs as A/2.5/Lo/64/Short and saved the two distinct JP2 references.
The application and independent receiver results are recorded in
`docs/sstv/HAMDRM_QSSTV_ORACLE.md`.

The regression checks exact A/B offline Decodium recovery and rejects swapped
references. These samples do not establish unmodified live transmitter, RF,
or general channel interoperability.
