## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 FT2 — v1.0.378**
👻 Smarter ghost filter on decodes directed to you

🛠 **What changed**
• The filter that hides "ghost" decodes (valid callsign but geographically impossible grid) now acts **only on low-confidence decodes** (the ones marked `?`). A decode directed to your callsign at **high confidence**, even with a corrupted grid, is no longer hidden: less risk of missing a real contact.
• Weak ghosts stay filtered as before.
• Cleaner diagnostic log: the same ghost is no longer logged dozens of times.
