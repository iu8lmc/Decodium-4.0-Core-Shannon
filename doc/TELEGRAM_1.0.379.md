## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 FT2 — v1.0.379**
🔧 Fix: QSO stalled with "Tx1" disabled

🛠 **What changed**
• If you keep **Tx1 disabled** (WSJT-X-style toggle) and double-click a station calling CQ, the reply now **starts from Tx2**, just like WSJT-X. Before, the sequencer stalled after the first transmission ("calls once, then freezes").
• You can now keep Tx1 off and answer normally. Applies to FT8, FT4 and FT2.

It was a regression introduced by the sequencer hardening in 1.0.375.
