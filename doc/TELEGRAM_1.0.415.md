## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 — v1.0.415**
⚡ Smoother FT4 (CPU-adaptive decoder) + custom Full Spectrum columns

🆕 **Smoother FT4**
• The FT4 decoder now **scales its effort to the CPU**: on lower-end PCs it skips the most expensive passes → **fewer stutters** in the waterfall and decodes, no more starving the audio within the 7.5s slot.
• On capable PCs **full sensitivity is kept** (the extra decodes stay on). Best of both.

✅ **Also includes the 1.0.414 changes (Full Spectrum)**
• **Freely draggable columns** left/right (grab the header and move it) + **resizable** from the divider line.
• Show/hide columns and order from the **right-click menu**.
• **JTDX-style decode counter**: the badge shows the **current period's** decodes (resets each cycle), not the running total.
