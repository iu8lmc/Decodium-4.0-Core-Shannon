## 🇮🇹 Italiano

📡 **Decodium 4 — v1.0.411**

🛠 **Cosa cambia**

## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 — v1.0.411**
🎛 Fully customizable Full Spectrum columns

🛠 **What changed**
• **Custom columns in Full Spectrum** — every column (UTC, dB, DT, Freq, Message, Dist, DXCC, Az) is now yours to arrange:
  – **Width** → drag the column's right edge.
  – **Right-click** the header → menu to **show/hide** columns, **move them left/right**, and **Reset**.
  – **"Newest on top"** → flips the list's chronological order.
• **Works in the detached Full Spectrum window too** — same layout, always consistent between the docked panel and the pop-out window.
• **Everything is saved** and restored on restart.

ℹ️ UI only: **the FT8/FT4/FT2 decoder and pipeline are untouched**.
