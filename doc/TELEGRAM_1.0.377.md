## 🇬🇧 English

📡 **Decodium 4 FT2 — v1.0.377**
🔌 CAT connection, logbook and stability

📻 **CAT / radio**
• More reliable auto-reconnect at startup: if you close Decodium with the rig connected, it reconnects on its own at the next launch (even without ticking "Auto-connect")

• Setup/Settings window usable on small monitors: adapts to the viewport, recenters, and OK/Cancel stay reachable

🔒 **Security & stability (carried from 1.0.376)**
• Crash fix on TX in FST4/FST4W/Fox/RTTY (waveform buffer)
• Crash fix on malformed WAV files
• Safer transmission (Digital Morse, CAT stop during TX) and PSK Reporter response cap
